Support

Recording, setup, purchase and account help for Flow Capture.

Updated October 14, 2026

This page provides help for the current preview of Flow Capture. A direct support contact will be added before the public App Store release.

Recording and comments

  • Record an iPhone app and review its screens
  • Add voice comments and review transcripts
  • Send screenshots and comments to Claude or Codex

Connect your computer

Follow MCP Setup to install the Mac connector, pair your iPhone and send a flow. The setup page also includes help for missing commands, expired pairing codes and disconnected computers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Open Settings → Account → Manage Subscription in Flow Capture to manage your subscription. You can also manage it in your iPhone’s Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For App Store billing issues or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support.

Delete an account or free up storage

Use Settings → Account → Delete Account to remove your Flow Capture cloud account and associated cloud data. Local projects remain on your iPhone. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Use Settings → Storage to review project sizes and delete videos or entire projects. Deleting only the video preserves screenshots and comments, but prevents recovering more frames from that video.

Prepare a problem report

In Flow Capture, open Settings → Report a Problem to prepare a report. Include what you expected, what happened and the steps to reproduce it. Keep the report until a support contact is available. Do not include passwords, pairing codes or private recordings.

Privacy and terms

Read the Privacy Policy for data handling and retention, and Terms of Use for the app license and purchase guidance.
