FAQ

Questions?
Start here.

For a closer look at recording and sharing, explore the guides →.

Can I comment with my voice?

Yes. You can narrate during a recording and add text comments to saved screens. New voice notes and cloud transcription depend on your plan and remaining allowance. Review comment placement and transcribe important audio before an AI handoff. Screen recording itself does not use transcription minutes. Read the voice guide.

What can I record or import?

Record an iPhone app journey, or import an existing recording or a set of screenshots. iOS or the source app may prevent capture of protected content.

How does Flow Capture decide which screens matter?

Screen selection runs on your iPhone. The app identifies stable states and reduces repeats. You review what it found, and the Frame Editor can recover a missed state from the original recording.

Does my recording leave the device?

Original recordings and saved projects stay on your iPhone. Screen selection runs locally. If you request voice transcription, comment polishing, direct Figma delivery, or MCP sharing, those optional services send the relevant audio, text, or export data for that action. Offline exports stay local until you share them.

What happens when I send a flow to Figma?

The companion plugin is designed to import screenshots as ordered image layers grouped by flow. It does not rebuild an app as native editable UI components. The plugin is being prepared for public release; a public Community download is not yet available.

Can I use the export with AI tools?

Yes. Use the MCP connection for direct delivery, or share an AI review ZIP without connecting a computer. After Send to AI, paste Copy Request into your Claude or Codex chat to start the review. Each send is a separate snapshot; it does not update an earlier export. Choose a handoff method.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 18 or later, with a visual design tuned for iOS 26.

Can I download Flow Capture now?

Not yet. The iPhone app and companion Figma plugin are being prepared for public release. We’ll replace this preview with a direct download link when they are available.
