FAQ

Questions and answers

Can I comment with my voice?

Yes. Speak while recording or write a note later. Each comment stays linked to the screen it belongs to, and you can export comments linked to screens or one continuous prompt for your AI handoff. Voice transcription is an optional cloud action you choose.

What can I record or import?

Any app on your iPhone. Record it directly, or import an existing screen recording, a video, or a set of screenshots.

How does Flow Capture decide which screens matter?

Screen selection runs on your iPhone. The app identifies stable states and reduces repeats. You review what it found, and the Frame Editor can recover a missed state from the original recording.

Does my recording leave the device?

Original recordings and saved projects stay on your iPhone. Screen selection runs locally. If you request voice transcription, comment polishing, or direct Figma delivery, those optional services send the relevant audio, text, or export data for that action. Offline exports stay local until you share them.

What happens when I send a flow to Figma?

The companion plugin imports screenshots as image layers grouped by flow, with names and order you can edit in Figma. It does not rebuild an app as native Figma UI components. Offline folder import is available without linking an account; direct delivery requires a connected account.

Can I use the export with AI tools?

Yes. The AI review ZIP includes a ready-to-use prompt, ordered screenshots, flow data, and any available text or voice transcripts. It is designed for Codex, Claude, and other tools that can read the package.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 18 or later, with a visual design tuned for iOS 26.

Can I download Flow Capture now?

Not yet. The iPhone app and companion Figma plugin are being prepared for public release. We’ll replace this preview with a direct download link when they are available.
